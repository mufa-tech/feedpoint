Four reasons to carry it

Trim from measurement

Type the resonance your analyzer shows. Feedpoint says how much to cut from each leg, learns this wire's real end effect, and uses it when you cut the next band.

Can I transmit here?

Frequency, mode and power against the rules for your country and class: US, Canada, UK and Germany. Every answer names its clause and the date the rules were checked.

RF exposure record

Minimum distances by the FCC method (OET-65), the German BNetzA method or the UK ICNIRP 2020 levels, saved with the date, the inputs and the sources, and shared as a PDF.

A field kit that works offline

Battery budget for your radio and mode, a one-hand log with a big UTC clock and a dupe check, ADIF 3.1.8 export, one file per park, and spot text ready to send by SMS.

Why we built it

The antenna and band plan apps hams use most have not been updated in years, the loggers sell a sync subscription, and outside English there is almost nothing. Feedpoint puts the sums you do on paper into one offline app, with the formula and the source under every result.
