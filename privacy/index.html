Privacy

Updated 2 November 2026.

Feedpoint collects no data. Nothing you type leaves your phone unless you share it yourself.

The app

Feedpoint has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads and no tracking. It makes no network calls of its own. Your stations, antennas, measurements, RF records, logs and settings are kept in one file inside the app on your phone.

It asks for no permissions: no location (you type your grid square), no camera, no microphone, no Bluetooth, no contacts.

Backups, ADIF and CSV files, PDF records and spot texts leave the phone only when you share or save them yourself, through the share sheet or the file picker, to a place you choose. We never receive them.

Purchases

Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. The store handles the payment under its own privacy terms; the app only learns that Pro is active and keeps that on the phone. This is the only time the app talks to a network.

Email

If you write to hello@mufatech.com, we read your message to answer it and keep it in our mailbox. We do not add you to any list.

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no third-party code; the fonts are served from this site. The calculators run in your browser and nothing you type is sent. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page changes first, with a new date at the top.
